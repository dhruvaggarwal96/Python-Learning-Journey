# Hotel Booking Demand Analysis | Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) with Python

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs a comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Hotel Booking Demand dataset to understand customer behavior, booking patterns, pricing strategies, and operational performance.

The analysis follows a structured data analysis workflow, including dataset understanding, feature exploration, data cleaning, visualization, business insights, and strategic recommendations. The goal is to transform raw booking data into actionable business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Hotel management needs to understand customer booking behavior, pricing trends, market demand, and reservation patterns to improve occupancy, optimize revenue, reduce cancellations, and enhance customer satisfaction.

This project answers key business questions through exploratory data analysis and provides recommendations that can help hotel management make informed decisions.

---

## 📂 Dataset Information

- **Dataset:** Hotel Booking Demand Dataset
- **Records:** ~119,000 hotel bookings
- **Features:** 32
- **Type:** Structured Tabular Data

The dataset contains information about:

- Hotel type
- Booking dates
- Customer demographics
- Meal preferences
- Reservation status
- Pricing (ADR)
- Market segments
- Customer type

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- City Hotels generated the highest number of bookings.
- A few countries contributed the majority of hotel reservations.
- ADR varied across hotel types, indicating different pricing strategies.
- Customers generally booked well in advance.
- BB (Bed & Breakfast) was the most preferred meal plan.
- Online Travel Agencies were the primary booking channel.
- Most customers were Transient guests.
- The majority of bookings resulted in successful check-outs.
- Repeat guests represented a relatively small proportion of customers.
- Most numerical features showed weak correlations, suggesting multiple factors influence booking behavior.
