# Day 33 — Real Data Analysis in SQL (Amazon Sales Case Study)

## 📌 Overview

Today's project focuses on solving real-world business problems using SQL. Instead of practicing isolated queries, I analyzed an Amazon-style sales dataset to answer business questions that data analysts commonly face in e-commerce.

The project covers sales performance, customer behavior, revenue analysis, and business decision-making by combining SQL queries with business insights and actionable conclusions.

---

## 🛠️ Database Setup

* Created a new database: `amazon_sales_db`
* Created a `sales` table
* Inserted sample Amazon sales data
* Performed business analysis using SQL

---

## 📊 Business Questions Solved

### ✅ Best Selling Product

* Identified the product with the highest quantity sold.
* Suggested inventory optimization and promotional strategies.

### ✅ Worst Selling Product

* Found the least-selling product.
* Recommended bundle offers, discounts, and demand analysis.

### ✅ Monthly Revenue Analysis

* Calculated revenue generated each month.
* Observed an increasing revenue trend and recommended analyzing growth factors.

### ✅ Top Customer

* Identified the highest revenue-generating customer.
* Suggested customer retention through loyalty programs and personalized offers.

### ✅ Customer Retention

* Counted customers who made repeat purchases.
* Discussed the importance of customer loyalty and repeat business.

### ✅ Average Basket Size

* Calculated the average number of items purchased per order.
* Suggested cross-selling, upselling, and bundle offers to increase basket size.

### ✅ Revenue by City

* Identified the highest revenue-generating city.
* Recommended focusing marketing, inventory, and logistics on high-performing regions.

### ✅ Revenue by Weekday

* Compared revenue across weekdays.
* Identified peak and low-performing days to improve promotions and operational planning.
