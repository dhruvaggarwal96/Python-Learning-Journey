# 💡 Key Business Insights

* Customers booking far in advance are more likely to cancel.
* City Hotels experience higher cancellation rates than Resort Hotels.
* Repeated guests are significantly more loyal.
* Families with children generate higher average room revenue.
* Previous cancellations strongly predict future cancellations.
* Guests with special requests are less likely to cancel.
* Non-refundable bookings have the highest cancellation rates.
* August receives the highest number of hotel bookings.
* Contract customers stay the longest on average.
* Online Travel Agencies generate the highest average ADR among market segments.

---

# 🧠 Key Learnings

* Every business question should begin with a hypothesis.
* Never draw conclusions without validating them using data.
* Correlation does not always imply causation.
* Business insights should be supported by measurable evidence.
* Always distinguish between metrics such as ADR, occupancy, and cancellation rate.
* Validate unusual values before making recommendations.
* Data analysis is about solving business problems, not just writing code.
