---

## 💡 Key Learning

One of the biggest takeaways today was understanding the difference between:

* **Order Value**
* **Average Order Value**
* **Total Revenue**
* **Average Category Revenue**

A small difference in the wording of a business question can completely change the SQL solution. Reading and understanding the requirement carefully is just as important as writing the query.
