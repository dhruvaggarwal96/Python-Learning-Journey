## 🧠 Key Concepts Learned

* Understood why Self Joins are required for hierarchical data such as Employee → Manager relationships.
* Learned the execution flow of scalar subqueries.
* Understood how nested subqueries execute from the innermost query outward.
* Learned how correlated subqueries execute once for every row of the outer query.
* Understood how `EXISTS` checks for the presence of at least one matching row.
* Learned how `NOT EXISTS` is used to find records with no matching related rows.
* Compared normal subqueries and correlated subqueries conceptually.
* Explored real-world business scenarios and interview-focused explanations.
